JobFill demo forms

Mock application forms that reproduce the markup patterns of popular applicant tracking systems. They are local test fixtures only and never submit anywhere. Load the extension from dist/, fill in your profile, then open a form and click Auto-Fill.

  • Greenhouse-style form — labelled inputs, React-Select style dropdowns, education repeater, EEO selects
  • Lever-style form — wrapped labels, link fields, radio & checkbox questions, EEO selects
  • Workday-style form — data-automation-id fields, button dropdowns, "Add" work/education sections, split month/year dates
  • Custom careers page — unlabeled divs, placeholders only, shadow DOM, numeric ranges, custom questions
