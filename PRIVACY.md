# Privacy Policy — JobFill

_Last updated: 2026-09-28_

JobFill is a browser extension that fills job application forms using information you enter.

## What data JobFill handles

- Information you type into the JobFill profile: name, contact details, address, links, work
  history, education, eligibility and preference answers, optional voluntary self-identification
  answers, and custom answers.
- Files you upload to JobFill: your resume and cover letter.
- Settings and, if enabled, a log of pages where you used Auto-Fill: page URL, page title,
  detected application platform, and counts of filled and empty fields.

## Where it is stored

Only on your device, in your Chrome profile's extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`). It is not
synced through your Google account and is removed if you uninstall the extension.

## What JobFill sends

Nothing, to us or to any third party. JobFill has no servers, analytics, advertising or remote
code. When, and only when, you click Auto-Fill, JobFill enters your information into the form on
the page you are viewing. What that website does with an application you submit is governed by
that website's privacy policy.

## Page access

JobFill's content script reads the structure of web pages to recognise job application forms and
show the Auto-Fill button. It does not record, store or transmit page content.

## Your control

Edit or delete any field at any time, export your data to a JSON file, use **Your data → Delete
all my data** to erase everything, and turn off the application log in Settings.
